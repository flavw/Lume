# LumeView Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

LumeView is a desktop image viewer developed by flavw. This policy describes how LumeView handles information.

## Local processing

LumeView processes images, comic archives, file names, folder contents and embedded metadata on your device to provide viewing, navigation, editing and metadata inspection. Metadata may include AI generation prompts, software details or camera information. This content is not uploaded to the developer.

LumeView does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking or automatic usage-reporting features. The application does not send your images, metadata or usage information to the developer.

## Files and settings

Preferences are saved locally. Image caches and temporary files may be created for viewing, archive extraction, conversion, editing or printing. Temporary files are normally removed when their owning operation or application session ends; an unexpected interruption may leave temporary files on the device. Saved or exported images remain in the location you select. Some image operations may preserve embedded metadata in their output.

File changes, exports and deletions occur in response to your actions. Files stored in a cloud-synchronized folder may be synchronized by your storage provider under its own privacy policy.

## Clipboard and printing

When you use copy or paste, LumeView reads or writes images or metadata through the system clipboard. Clipboard synchronization or clipboard history, if enabled in your operating system, is controlled by the operating system.

When you choose Print, LumeView provides an image to the Windows printing system. Printing and any transmission to a selected network printer or printing service are handled by Windows and your printer configuration.

## Third-party services

LumeView does not sell your information or share it with advertising providers. Microsoft Store, Windows, cloud storage providers and printer services operate independently and may process information according to their own policies. The developer does not control those services.

If you voluntarily contact the developer or submit a GitHub issue, the information you provide is processed to respond to your request. GitHub issues are public; do not include private images or sensitive information in a public issue.

## Your choices

You control which files you open and which content you save, copy or print. You can remove locally saved files and application preferences through your operating system. No developer-hosted account or image storage is created by LumeView.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the application's data handling changes. Updates will be reflected in the date above.

For privacy questions, contact flavw through the project issue tracker: https://github.com/flavw/LumeView/issues
